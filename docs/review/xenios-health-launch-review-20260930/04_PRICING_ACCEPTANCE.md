## Monetary precision and rounding contract: a finding

- The workbook's raw "Suggested Sell Price" has sub-cent values in **67 rows**. Of these:
  - 48 are Care (503A)
  - 11 are supplements
  - 5 are research-use vials
  - 1 is a topical
- The workbook's `Price Display` column rounds **half to even**: 2.625 → $2.62, 153.125 → $153.12.
- Production cents came from `scripts/research/initialize-general-product-control.ts:102`, which applies
  `Math.round(amount * 100)`. That is **half up** on the binary float.
- Results on purchasable (non-Care) rows:
  - **Ipamorelin 5 mg (GRP-0303):** book display $153.12, stored $153.13.
  - **GRP-0371:** book display $108.12, stored $108.13.
  - **GRP-0379:** book display $43.12, stored $43.13.
  - **Tesamorelin 5 mg (GRP-0348):** the book exports `169.27499999999998`, a binary artifact of 169.275. Half-up of
    the intended value is $169.28, but $169.27 is stored. The float truncates it, so production follows **neither
    rule consistently**.
- 1¢ differences in total: 16 rows (half-up versus display), plus 1 artifact row.
- **Required of the successor:**
  - Name one authoritative contract: approved cents are the active Product Control row, and conversion from the
    book is by a stated rule on the exact decimal.
  - Never parse formatted CSV text.
  - Never apply `Math.round` to a float carrying binary artifacts.
  - Produce a founder-visible list of the 17 affected rows, so they can confirm the cents to publish.
  - Do not change a live price without a separately authorized Product Control release.

## Pricing findings added to the register

- **HL-18 · P2 · Rounding contract undefined and inconsistent** (above).
  - Correction: exact-decimal conversion, a declared rule, and founder confirmation of the 17 rows.
- **HL-19 · P3 · A shipping charge is served as a catalog product.** FedEx Standard Overnight (GRP-0364) is a served
  variant in `care_pathway`, with no binding. It should belong to fulfillment pricing, not the catalog.
- **HL-20 · P2 · The same variant has two different totals depending on which door the customer uses.**
  - Seams:
    - *Featured* cards route to the legacy Early Access order service, which applies the versioned 20%
      three-unit bundle promotion (`early-access/commerce/order-service.ts:67,152`; `promotion.ts`, integer basis
      points, floored).
    - *All products* routes to the assisted order, whose estimate is a plain sum
      (`shared/research/assisted-order/contract.ts:729-744`; `assisted-order/service.ts:476`).
    - Featured copy says: "Assisted order requests support 1–100 units … Quantity 3 receives the server-confirmed
      Research Bundle pricing." (`EarlyAccessCatalogSection.tsx:133-137`).
  - Consequence: three units of the same variant produce a 20% different estimate depending on the door, and the
    copy attributes bundle pricing to assisted requests.
  - Correction: one server pricing function (with promotion) for both paths, or remove the promotion claim from the
    assisted context.
