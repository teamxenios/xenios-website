CORE MODULES

The operating pieces behind the relationship.
View Product
One inbox

Client messages, check-ins, reminders, and follow-ups land in one queue instead of five tabs.

One client record

Goals, history, notes, programs, context, and progress stay attached to the person, not scattered across tools.

Xen drafts in your voice

The professional-facing agent prepares the check-in, reply, or next action. You approve before it reaches the client.

The Studio

The Client Attention Queue shows who needs you today, why they were flagged, and what action is ready for review.

Hercules

The weekly check-in engine prepares client updates in your voice, grounded in each client's actual week.

Approval gate

Nothing client-facing goes out unless the coach approves it. The AI drafts. The coach decides.
