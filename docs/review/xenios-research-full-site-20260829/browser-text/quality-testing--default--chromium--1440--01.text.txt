THE XENIOS QUALITY SYSTEM

Evidence travels with the lot.

A certificate is one record in a larger control system. The material, exact lot, review decision, approved documents, storage, and fulfillment trail must remain connected.

A certificate of analysis or purity result does not, by itself, establish identity, potency, sterility, safety, stability, or suitability. Testing and review requirements vary by material and intended research context.

LOT CONTROL, END TO END

From receiving to a traceable decision.

Each step preserves a different fact. No step silently substitutes for another, and an unavailable record is never presented as a passing result.

Receive

Inbound material is matched to the expected shipment and receiving record before it enters usable inventory.

Inspect

Packaging, quantity, labeling, condition, and shipment exceptions are recorded against the received material.

Identify the lot

The exact SKU and supplier lot are bound to a controlled internal lot record so later evidence cannot drift to another batch.

Quarantine

Material remains outside released inventory while required documentation and applicable test evidence are reviewed.

Review evidence

Required documents and third-party testing, where applicable, are checked for lot identity, method, sample, dates, issuing party, specification, and exceptions—not a headline number alone.

Decide

An authorized review records release, hold, continued quarantine, or withdrawal. Missing evidence is never treated as a pass.

Publish approved records

Only explicitly approved public summaries, COAs, and supporting documents may appear in lot verification. Private source records remain private.

Store and fulfill

Released inventory remains lot-traceable through storage, reservation, fulfillment, and any documented deviation, exception, or withdrawal.

DECISION INTEGRITY

A missing record is a stop, not a shortcut.
Release

Required evidence is present and the recorded review supports movement into released inventory for its authorized use.

Hold or quarantine

Missing, inconsistent, damaged, or unresolved material stays out of released inventory while the exception is investigated.

Trace through fulfillment

The exact lot remains connected to storage, reservation, fulfillment, and any later withdrawal or exception record.

When damage, a temperature excursion, a documentation gap, a complaint, or another deviation is recorded, the event stays attached to the exact lot’s exception trail. Investigation, disposition, and corrective or preventive action belong there when the event requires them.

PUBLIC LOT RECORDS

Start with the code on the label.

Verification returns only an exact, approved public match. It does not search approximately, expose a private source file, or treat a source outage as “not found.”
