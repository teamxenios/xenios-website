HOW IT WORKS

From first question to a defensible next action.

Every step is designed to preserve the difference between interest, access, a request, an order, payment, fulfillment, Care, and completion.

STEP 01

Choose a pathway

Start with Research, private Early Access, Care, an organization, a partner relationship, supplier access, or support. Do not force a clinical need through a Research workflow.

STEP 02

Verify access

Sign in or follow the applicable review process. Membership, organization roles, partner status, supplier assignments, and administrative authority are resolved by the server.

STEP 03

Review exact facts

Confirm exact product and variant identity, format, documentation status, access state, and any current action before submitting a request.

STEP 04

Request through the authorized path

A live action, assisted request, availability inquiry, provider handoff, documentation-pending state, hold, or unavailable state each behaves differently.

STEP 05

Complete required review

Provide only the contact, shipping, and acknowledgments required by the selected workflow. A submitted request is not called an order or a successful payment unless durable evidence supports it.

STEP 06

Follow source-aware status

Account views are designed to separate requests, orders, payment, refunds, fulfillment, tracking, Care availability, documents, and support. A missing source must remain unknown or unavailable.

A FIRM BOUNDARY

Research access is not clinical care.

Research-designated materials are for legitimate nonclinical research only. Diagnosis, treatment, prescribing, individualized guidance, clinical records, and pharmacy activity belong to authorized providers and systems in the separate Care pathway.
