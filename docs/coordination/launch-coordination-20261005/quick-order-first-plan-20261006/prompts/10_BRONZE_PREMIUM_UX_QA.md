## Design reference

Use the accepted A-E/S1-S7 direction, not the old drifted founder preview:
Xenios Health visible public header/footer; existing Xenios Care and Research
names; restrained purple focus/selected accents and teal support; black
rectangular customer actions; square contain media; no admin restyling by the

Premium means legible hierarchy, consistent spacing, confident truthful copy,
clear steps and error recovery. It does not mean animation, invented packaging,
hype, gradients everywhere or hiding mandatory controls.

## Quick Order examination

Review the actual mounted candidate at 320, 390, 768, 1024, 1280 and 1440 pixels.
Record measured viewport width, not just requested size. Test keyboard, visible
focus, labels, errors/summary focus, zoom at 200%, long product names/addresses,
loading/empty/refused states, review/edit/back and network recovery. Test native
forced-colors where available and mark emulation separately. Reserve media
space, check layout shift and avoid sticky elements hiding the submit control.

Check the distinction between request-only, pending price, unavailable and Care.
Provider-only products must not display Research bundle/quantity/purchase copy.
Do not mark an illustrative study as verified packaging. No missing-image promise
that an asset is on its way. Confirm the existing PWA install prompt does not
obscure intake once its correction is authorized and applied.

Check form privacy: personal details and declared sources do not appear in URL,
analytics, browser storage or screenshot evidence. Use synthetic customers only.
Successful request copy cannot say paid, approved, shipped or treatment granted.

## Full-site check

Use the actual public shell, catalog, member detail, partner entry, order/status
and support routes. Trace links, direct navigation, reload, back, stale session
and an unavailable backend. Flag inconsistent real code; do not quietly choose
a new visual policy in a standalone mockup.

## Output economy

Deliver at most one prioritized list per candidate, with screenshot/route/
viewport/reproduction and the smallest expected correction. Distinguish blockers
from optional polish. Do not count multiple viewport manifestations as separate
business defects unless their fixes differ. Avoid another hundreds-row drift
matrix for unchanged accepted UI.

No browser run without the shared reservation. Static review can proceed while
compute is reserved elsewhere. Screenshots are evidence, not approval of source,
product truth, legal applicability or deployment. No new rendering or stock-image
purchase needed for the first intake release.
