  - paragraph: Partners
  - heading "Account security" [level=1]
  - paragraph: The basics that keep your rep account yours. Most of this is habits; the rest is knowing what the team will never ask for.
  - region "The basics":
    - heading "The basics" [level=2]
    - paragraph: One account, one person
    - paragraph: Your rep account is yours alone. Sharing credentials, running someone else's account, or operating multiple personal accounts is grounds for removal.
    - paragraph: A strong, unique password
    - paragraph: Use a password you use nowhere else, ideally from a password manager. If you suspect it is known to anyone, change it immediately.
    - paragraph: We never ask for credentials
    - paragraph: No one from the team will ever ask for your password, a sign-in code, or payout credentials by email, text, or phone. Treat any such request as an attack.
    - paragraph: Sign out on shared devices
    - paragraph: If you sign in on a device that is not yours, sign out when you are done. Your dashboard and ledger are nobody else's business.
    - paragraph: Report anything odd, fast
    - paragraph: "Unexpected sign-in notices, changed details you did not change, or messages pretending to be the team: report them the moment you see them."
