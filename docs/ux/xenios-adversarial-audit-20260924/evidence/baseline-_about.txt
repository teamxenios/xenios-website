- main:
  - paragraph: ABOUT
  - heading "The operating layer for proactive health relationships." [level=1]
  - paragraph: xenios exists because the trusted relationship between a professional and a client is where behavior changes, but that relationship is currently held together by messages, spreadsheets, notes, forms, and memory.
  - paragraph: MISSION
  - heading "Give great professionals more leverage without thinning the relationship." [level=2]
  - paragraph: The current tool stack fragments the client relationship. Programming lives in one place, messages in another, notes somewhere else, billing somewhere else, and follow-up in the professional's head.
  - paragraph: AI can extend the professional when it is bounded by context, approval, and trust. The AI drafts. The coach decides.
  - paragraph: TEAM
  - heading "A small team built for the problem." [level=2]
  - article:
    - heading "Samuel Boadu" [level=3]
    - paragraph: Founder and CEO
    - paragraph: "Samuel is building from the inside of the coaching and performance world. The founding insight is simple: client relationships change behavior, but the operating layer behind those relationships is broken."
  - article:
    - heading "Brian Bentow" [level=3]
    - paragraph: CTO
    - paragraph: Brian brings senior healthcare and payments technology experience, including prior exits and the infrastructure judgment needed for sensitive workflows.
  - paragraph: MARKET THESIS
  - heading "The proactive health professional is underserved by legacy tools." [level=2]
  - paragraph: Coaches, trainers, dietitians, clinicians, and performance staff are doing the work upstream of disease. Their tools were built for scheduling, notes, content, or billing. Not for the relationship itself.
  - paragraph: xenios is pre-seed, MVP-building, and forming the early cohort. The goal is not to pretend the company is mature. The goal is to build the right system with the right first professionals.
