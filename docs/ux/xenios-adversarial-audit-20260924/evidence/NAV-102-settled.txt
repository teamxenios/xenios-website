- main:
  - paragraph: The Xenios quality system
  - heading "Evidence travels with the lot." [level=1]
  - paragraph: A certificate is one record in a larger control system. The material, exact lot, review decision, approved documents, storage, and fulfillment trail must remain connected.
  - paragraph: A certificate of analysis or purity result does not, by itself, establish identity, potency, sterility, safety, stability, or suitability. Testing and review requirements vary by material and intended research context.
  - region "From receiving to a traceable decision.":
    - paragraph: Lot control, end to end
    - heading "From receiving to a traceable decision." [level=2]
    - paragraph: Each step preserves a different fact. No step silently substitutes for another, and an unavailable record is never presented as a passing result.
    - article:
      - heading "Receive" [level=3]
      - paragraph: Inbound material is matched to the expected shipment and receiving record before it enters usable inventory.
    - article:
      - heading "Inspect" [level=3]
      - paragraph: Packaging, quantity, labeling, condition, and shipment exceptions are recorded against the received material.
    - article:
      - heading "Identify the lot" [level=3]
      - paragraph: The exact SKU and supplier lot are bound to a controlled internal lot record so later evidence cannot drift to another batch.
    - article:
      - heading "Quarantine" [level=3]
      - paragraph: Material remains outside released inventory while required documentation and applicable test evidence are reviewed.
    - article:
      - heading "Review evidence" [level=3]
      - paragraph: Required documents and third-party testing, where applicable, are checked for lot identity, method, sample, dates, issuing party, specification, and exceptions—not a headline number alone.
    - article:
      - heading "Decide" [level=3]
      - paragraph: An authorized review records release, hold, continued quarantine, or withdrawal. Missing evidence is never treated as a pass.
    - article:
      - heading "Publish approved records" [level=3]
      - paragraph: Only explicitly approved public summaries, COAs, and supporting documents may appear in lot verification. Private source records remain private.
    - article:
      - heading "Store and fulfill" [level=3]
      - paragraph: Released inventory remains lot-traceable through storage, reservation, fulfillment, and any documented deviation, exception, or withdrawal.
  - region "A missing record is a stop, not a shortcut.":
    - paragraph: Decision integrity
    - heading "A missing record is a stop, not a shortcut." [level=2]
    - article:
      - heading "Release" [level=3]
      - paragraph: Required evidence is present and the recorded review supports movement into released inventory for its authorized use.
    - article:
      - heading "Hold or quarantine" [level=3]
      - paragraph: Missing, inconsistent, damaged, or unresolved material stays out of released inventory while the exception is investigated.
    - article:
      - heading "Trace through fulfillment" [level=3]
      - paragraph: The exact lot remains connected to storage, reservation, fulfillment, and any later withdrawal or exception record.
    - paragraph: When damage, a temperature excursion, a documentation gap, a complaint, or another deviation is recorded, the event stays attached to the exact lot’s exception trail. Investigation, disposition, and corrective or preventive action belong there when the event requires them.
  - region "Start with the code on the label.":
    - paragraph: Public lot records
    - heading "Start with the code on the label." [level=2]
    - paragraph: Verification returns only an exact, approved public match. It does not search approximately, expose a private source file, or treat a source outage as “not found.”
