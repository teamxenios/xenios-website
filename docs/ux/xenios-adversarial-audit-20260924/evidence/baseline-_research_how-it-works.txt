    - heading "Complete the required information" [level=2]

    - paragraph: Step 04
    - heading "Receive the appropriate review" [level=2]
    - paragraph: A U.S.-licensed clinician reviews Care. Research actions are governed by Product Control, documentation, availability, and account authority.
    - paragraph: Step 05
    - heading "Continue only when authorized" [level=2]
    - paragraph: A clinician may prescribe when clinically appropriate. A pharmacy may fulfill when serviceable. A Research request or order proceeds only when its own gates pass.
    - paragraph: Step 06
    - heading "Follow status and support" [level=2]
    - paragraph: Use the appropriate account, provider, pharmacy, Research, and support sources for updates, documents, tracking, follow-up, and exceptions.
