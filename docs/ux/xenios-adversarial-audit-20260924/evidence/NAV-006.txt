    - list:
      - text: "01"
      - heading "Submit a Care access request" [level=3]
      - paragraph: Provide contact information, current U.S. state, a broad routing category, and contact preference. The public form collects no clinical free text.
      - text: "02"
      - heading "Receive human follow-up" [level=3]
      - paragraph: A Xenios team member reviews the operational request and contacts you, typically within one business day.
      - text: "03"
      - heading "Move to a secure clinical system when appropriate" [level=3]
      - paragraph: If a serviceable option is available, complete any identity, consent, health questionnaire, records, or scheduling steps only in the authorized secure system provided to you.
      - text: "04"
      - heading "Receive independent clinical review" [level=3]
      - paragraph: A licensed clinician determines whether more information, records, labs, a visit, a different approach, treatment, or no treatment is appropriate. No outcome or prescription is guaranteed.
