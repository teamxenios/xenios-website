  - paragraph: SECURITY POSTURE
  - heading "Data handling" [level=3]
  - paragraph: Client context is treated as sensitive business and health-adjacent data. The record is designed to keep access scoped to the professional and approved team members.
  - heading "AI access boundaries" [level=3]
  - paragraph: Xen can reference approved client context to prepare drafts. Athena is disclosed as AI and stays under the professional's boundaries.
  - heading "Approval before delivery" [level=3]
  - paragraph: Version one keeps client-facing output behind professional review. The AI drafts. The coach decides.
  - heading "Encryption" [level=3]
  - paragraph: TLS in transit and encryption at rest through infrastructure providers. Technical details are available during security review.
  - heading "Access control" [level=3]
  - paragraph: Role-based access, scoped accounts, and audit-oriented workflows are part of the product posture.
  - heading "Incident response" [level=3]
  - paragraph: Security issues should be sent directly to the team. We prioritize rapid review, containment, and clear communication.
  - paragraph: DATA FLOW
  - heading "Plain-language architecture." [level=2]
  - paragraph: The goal is to make data movement understandable to a solo coach and substantive enough for a compliance reviewer.
  - generic: "01"
  - paragraph: Client messages, check-ins, notes, and signals enter the workspace.
  - generic: "02"
  - paragraph: The client record keeps context attached to the person and the professional relationship.
  - generic: "03"
  - paragraph: Xen prepares drafts, summaries, or next actions from approved context.
  - generic: "04"
  - paragraph: The professional reviews before anything client-facing is delivered.
  - generic: "05"
  - paragraph: Actions, edits, and updates are retained as part of the operating record.
