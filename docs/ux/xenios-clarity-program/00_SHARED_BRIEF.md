# XENIOS / INFINITY SHARED EXECUTION BRIEF
**Controlling product brief for Claude Code and OpenAI Codex**

## 1. Objective

Create a public Xenios experience that is as easy to understand and act on as the strongest consumer-health platforms, while supporting a much broader business:

- direct consumers
- clinician-guided Care
- Research/product ordering
- clinics and professional practices
- coaches and wellness professionals
- affiliates and referral partners
- suppliers, pharmacies, laboratories, and fulfillment partners
- careers
- existing customers, partners, operators, and administrators

A visitor must understand where they fit within 30 seconds.

## 4. Stephen Toth / Compass practice reference

The Stephen/Compass meeting is the principal B2B2C use case.

A practice like Compass may need:

1. Referral model
   - practice refers the client
   - client creates and owns the customer account
   - practice attribution is preserved
   - approved commission/reporting is visible
   - client remains associated with the originating practice
   - Care is not implied unless selected

2. Practice/organization account
   - organization profile
   - authorized staff
   - linked clients
   - referral links/codes
   - order and fulfillment visibility
   - commission/payout reporting
   - support and account ownership

3. Care integration
   - client completes the correct Care intake
   - licensed clinician makes independent decisions
   - provider/practice relationship and follow-up responsibilities are explicit
   - no automatic prescribing or practice liability is implied

4. Wholesale/inventory relationship
   - only if legally, clinically, contractually, and operationally supported
   - separate from referrals and Care
   - requires explicit authority and documentation

5. Education/support
   - product information
   - fulfillment and status
   - account support
   - safe educational content
   - no unapproved dosing or clinical instructions from a generic chatbot

## 7. Core UX contract

Every accepted action must answer:

1. What did I just do?
2. Did the system receive it?
3. What is the current status?
4. What happens next?
5. Who owns the next step?
6. Where do I return?
7. Will I receive a confirmation?

Never use these as synonyms:
- inquiry
- application
- access request
- approval
- activation
- sign-in
- Care request
- order

## 8. Product-state contract

Every visible product must be one of:

- Care
- Research order
- Assisted order
- Unavailable

Every card must show:
- exact product
- exact format/variant
- simple purpose
- price or honest price status
- availability
- pathway
- one CTA

No product may appear purchasable and then dead-end.

## 9. Practice model contract

The “For Practices” experience must answer:

- What kind of practice is this for?
- Can I refer clients?
- Do clients create their own accounts?
- Can my staff have access?
- How is attribution tracked?
- How are commissions calculated and shown?
- Who owns the client relationship?
- What does Xenios handle?
- What does the practice handle?
- When is Care required?
- Can a practice place orders?
- What legal/clinical authority is required?
- What happens next after an inquiry?

## 10. Authority and safety boundaries

Preserve:
- server-side authentication
- customer/partner/admin owner isolation
- Care/clinical independence
- PHI restrictions
- Research/Care distinction
- manual-payment verification
- notification/outbox integrity
- native commerce disabled unless separately authorized
- exact legal document gates
- no false submission
- no invented pricing, serviceability, pharmacy, clinician, shipping, or testing claims

## 11. Definition of done

The redesign is not complete because the page renders or tests pass.

It is complete when:
- a first-time visitor understands the company
- each audience knows where to go
- each CTA means one thing
- supported products show a truthful price/pathway
- accepted submissions create server evidence
- users receive truthful next steps
- human operators receive obligations
- customers can return to status
- mobile and desktop work
- Stephen/Compass can understand the practice model without a private explanation
- Samuel does not need to interpret the website for users
