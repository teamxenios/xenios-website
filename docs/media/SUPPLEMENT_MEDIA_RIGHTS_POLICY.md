# Supplement Media Rights Policy

Official product discovery and permission to republish are separate facts. A publicly reachable product page is evidence of product identity, not a media license.

## Rights states

Only `SUPPLIER_PROVIDED_APPROVED`, `BRAND_MEDIA_PORTAL_APPROVED`, `AUTHORIZED_RESELLER_USE`, `WRITTEN_PERMISSION_APPROVED`, and `ORIGINAL_XENIOS_RENDER` may proceed toward public use. `OFFICIAL_SOURCE_RIGHTS_PENDING` and `INTERNAL_REVIEW_ONLY` remain non-public. `DO_NOT_USE` is excluded from download, transformation, upload, and linkage.

Every approved state must retain the evidence reference, granting person or organization, permission date, expiration when applicable, and limitations. Credentials and secret values are never stored in the media manifest.

## Publication gate

Public linkage requires all of the following:

1. Exact variant match.
2. Approved rights state.
3. Manual Product Control approval.
4. Exact canonical variant identifier.
5. Source URL, storage path, public URL, and factual alt text.

The system does not treat another flavor, count, size, formulation, or historical label as a valid fallback.
