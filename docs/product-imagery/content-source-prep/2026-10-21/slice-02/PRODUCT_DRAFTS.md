## NUT-006 / R149C

### Card summary draft

NutriDyn Chondro Jointaide combines three capsule formulas in individually packed portions.

### Product detail draft

Each packet brings together one Chondro-Relief capsule, two Chondro-Relief Intensive Care capsules and two Inflam-Eze Plus capsules. The format keeps the different formulas together rather than presenting them as a single uniform capsule. The listed package contains 60 packets, not 60 capsules. Its ingredients include glucosamine, chondroitin, MSM and plant-derived constituents. This is a shellfish-containing product.

### Private source facts and reconciliation

- Listed servings: 30; the ingredient panel describes two packets, not one.
- The page identifies crab and shrimp and specifically excludes use by people with shellfish allergy. Preserve that warning in label review; do not describe the product as allergy-safe.
- The component ingredient lists include gelatin. Do not substitute a vegetarian capsule identity.
- Exact current supplied package and complete component panels remain unverified. No substitute joint-formula photo is acceptable merely because its name is similar.

[Manufacturer product page](https://nutridyn.com/chondro-jointaide).

## NUT-007 / R190

### Card summary draft

NutriDyn Collagen Renew is an unflavored bovine collagen peptide powder, supplied in a canister.

### Product detail draft

The formula combines four named peptide ingredients: Fortigel, Fortibone, Tendoforte and Verisol. It is an animal-derived powder, distinct from a plant-based supplement or a collagen tablet. The source separately reports protein content and collagen-peptide mass; those figures describe different label entries, not quantities to add together.

### Private source facts and reconciliation

- Listed servings: 30. Panel basis: approximately one scoop, 17.5 g; collagen peptides 17.5 g and protein 15 g.
- Repository name includes "Dynamic Multi"; the manufacturer heading is "Collagen Renew". The exact R190 match supports a provisional alias, not a second product.
- The summary form field is blank, while the page describes scooping from a canister. Net package weight is not confirmed; do not calculate it from serving count.
- The linked technical sheet was not inspected in this slice. No packaging revision or full label reconciliation is claimed.

[Manufacturer product page](https://nutridyn.com/dynamic-multi-collagen-renew).

## NUT-009 / R222

### Card summary draft

NutriDyn UltraBiotic Prebiotic is a cranberry-lemonade-flavored powder with XOS, kiwi and fruit-derived ingredients.

### Product detail draft

The composition combines xylooligosaccharides with organic gold kiwi powder, pomegranate and cranberry extracts, and polyphenols from red-wine fruit extract. Its prebiotic identity should remain distinct from products described by live-culture strains and CFU counts: neither a strain list nor a CFU count is declared on this source page. Additional ingredients include stevia leaf extract and flavoring.

### Private source facts and reconciliation

- Listed servings: approximately 30. Panel basis: approximately one scoop, 6.84 g; XOS 1.4 g and kiwi powder 1.2 g.
- The summary form field is blank; the scoop-based description establishes the provisional powder format. Net package weight remains unconfirmed and must not be inferred by multiplication.
- No refrigeration requirement is inferred from the UltraBiotic name.
- The linked technical sheet was not inspected. Full current panel and supplied label revision need reconciliation.

[Manufacturer product page](https://nutridyn.com/ultrabiotic-prebiotic).
