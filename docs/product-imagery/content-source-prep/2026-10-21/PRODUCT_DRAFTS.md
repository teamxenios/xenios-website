## NUT-002 / R227

### Card summary draft

Magtein is NutriDyn's capsule-format magnesium supplement using magnesium L-threonate.

### Product detail draft

This formula identifies the magnesium compound rather than grouping different magnesium products together. Its label distinguishes the amount of elemental magnesium from the larger amount of magnesium L-threonate that supplies it. Those two numbers describe different quantities and should not be added together. The additional capsule ingredients are listed below.

### Private label facts

- Package: 90 capsules; 30 labeled servings.
- Panel basis: 3 capsules; magnesium 144 mg, supplied by 2 g magnesium L-threonate.
- Additional ingredients: hypromellose, rice flour, vegetable stearic acid, silica.
- Listed brand: NutriDyn. Legal manufacturer/facility: not independently confirmed.

## NUT-003 / R167

### Card summary draft

Mito Recharge is a NutriDyn capsule supplement combining vitamins, minerals and other nutritional ingredients, including CoQ10 and PQQ.

### Product detail draft

This is a multi-ingredient nutritional formula, not a single-compound product. Its composition includes acetyl-L-carnitine, taurine, plant-derived constituents and two separately listed forms of alpha-lipoic acid. The manufacturer identifies the product with cellular-energy nutrition; that description is not evidence of a treatment effect or a guaranteed outcome.

### Private label facts

- Package: 90 capsules; 30 labeled servings; panel basis 3 capsules.
- Panel constituents: vitamins C, E, K1, B1, B2, B3 and B6; magnesium; selenium; taurine; acetyl-L-carnitine HCl; resveratrol; coenzyme Q10; N-acetyl-L-cysteine; EGCG; quercetin; alpha-lipoic acid; R-alpha-lipoic acid; sulforaphane glucosinolate; hydroxytyrosol; PQQ; mixed tocopherols.
- Additional ingredients: hypromellose, vegetable magnesium stearate, silica.
- Exact constituent amounts, chemical forms and complete label text require panel reconciliation before a complete specification is claimed. The abbreviated list is not a substitute for the Supplement Facts panel.
- Listed brand: NutriDyn. Legal manufacturer/facility: not independently confirmed.

## NUT-005 / R817E

### Card summary draft

Omega Pure EPA-DHA 2400 is NutriDyn's lemon-flavored liquid fish-oil supplement, distinct from its softgel products.

### Product detail draft

This liquid contains EPA and DHA in triglyceride form, together with other omega-3 fatty acids. The listed EPA and DHA amounts add to 2,400 mg, distinct from total omega-3 content. Match the liquid package when selecting a photograph; a softgel bottle from another Omega Pure variant does not represent this item.

### Private label facts

- Package: 150 mL; approximately 30 labeled servings.
- Panel basis: 5 mL; EPA 1,410 mg; DHA 990 mg; other omega-3s 400 mg; total omega-3s 2.8 g.
- Ingredients: fish oil from anchovy, sardine and mackerel; lemon flavor; tocopherols, rosemary extract and ascorbyl palmitate.
- Allergen: fish. Not the Omega Pure 720 softgel product.
- Listed brand: NutriDyn. Legal manufacturer/facility: not independently confirmed.

[Manufacturer product page](https://nutridyn.com/omega-pure-epa-dha-2400). The panel facts above were cross-checked against page 2 of the [manufacturer technical sheet, revision 2/25](https://nutridyn.com/media/productsattachment/Omega_Pure_EPA-DHA_2400_SS_ND417_NEW.pdf).
