Medium biography · approval draft

Ehinomen “Hollywood Hino” Ehikhamenor is a Nigeria-born former professional cruiserweight, The Contender Season 4 runner-up, and boxing-based fitness trainer. Raised in LeFrak City, Queens, he built a 15–4 professional record before moving into coaching, fitness programming, entrepreneurship, and the King Hino brand. The U.S. Small Business Administration later featured him in its My Brother's Keeper Millennial Entrepreneurs series.
