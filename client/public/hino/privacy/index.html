Approval draft · Privacy

Collect only what the path requires.

This approval-stage website does not transmit or store contact-form submissions, set marketing cookies, or operate analytics. Final privacy language must match the approved production systems before launch.

Preview data behavior

The inquiry form validates in the browser and stops there. No message, email address, name, or location is sent to a server. External links are operated by their respective destinations and their own privacy terms.

Embedded media

YouTube players use a click-to-load privacy gate. No player request is made until a visitor chooses to load a video. Loading or playing a privacy-enhanced YouTube embed can still share technical connection and playback data with YouTube under its own privacy terms. Instagram is link-only in this preview; native posts are not loaded.

Production requirements

Before launch, identify the legal operator, contact destination, form processor, analytics tools, retention period, user-rights procedure, cookie behavior, and each third-party processor. Do not collect health information through the general inquiry form.
