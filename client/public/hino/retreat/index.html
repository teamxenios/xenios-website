The four-day journey

Arrive. Work. Reflect. Move forward.

Session times are not published. The sequence below preserves the current official program while removing duplicated source copy.

FridayArrival and brotherhood begins+
  • Check-in and welcome reception
  • Welcome mixer
  • Welcome dinner and introductions: why each attendee is present and what he wants to reset
  • Fireside conversation: purpose, success, family, responsibility, legacy
  • Bonfire, scheduled cigar time, quiet reflection, free time, and late-night conversation
SaturdayMind, body, and brotherhood+
  • Morning workout and mobility: stretching, boxing, strength, hiking, or functional fitness
  • Healthy breakfast
  • Men's conversation: mental health, fatherhood, relationships, career growth, leadership pressure, identity after 40, emotional strength, and personal responsibility
  • Lunch and recharge
  • Open leisure and brotherhood time: optional golf, spa, fishing, pool time, rest, journaling, cigars, or networking
  • Dress to Impress dinner
  • Guest speaker and entrepreneur spotlight: discipline, leadership, business, adversity, failure, adaptation, and achievement
  • Brotherhood social with cigars, music, cards, laughter, and conversation
SundayRecharge, reflect, and plan+
  • Morning movement: nature walk, yoga, stretching, breathwork, or meditation
  • Breakfast
  • Goal-setting workshop: health, finances, family, relationships, personal growth, purpose, and legacy
  • Accountability-partner session
  • Lunch and personal recharge: rest, journaling, spa, walking, quiet time, or one-to-one conversation
  • Brotherhood activities: sports, pool, games, networking, cigars, and relaxed connection
  • Dinner
  • Open mic and story circle
  • Cigar lounge, bonfire, and late-night conversation
MondayReset and move forward+
  • Final morning workout and meditation
  • Breakfast
  • Personal 90-day action-plan workshop: health commitments, daily habits, discipline structure, family leadership, financial direction, purpose and legacy, personal accountability, and next steps
  • Group commitments and closing remarks
  • Professional group photo
  • Farewell brunch
  • Departure

The setting

Amish Country, Ohio.

Space away from daily noise is part of the design. The exact TreeBox property, address, room configuration, arrival airport, and transfer instructions are not published and must be confirmed by the operator.

Included

The official program.

  • Three nights' accommodations at the operator-described TreeBox property
  • Round-trip ground transportation during the retreat
  • Meals as outlined in the official program
  • Welcome reception, mixer, and welcome dinner
  • Daily fitness, mobility, and wellness sessions
  • Leadership, financial-wellness, and planning workshops
  • Men's empowerment panel discussions
  • Entrepreneur and leadership guest-speaker sessions
  • Accountability-partner program
  • Brotherhood activities, bonfires, and evening gatherings
  • Select cigar experiences during scheduled social events

Not included

Plan for the rest.

  • Gratuities, incidentals, room service, and personal expenses
  • Medical expenses and emergency services
  • Anything not expressly listed as included

Before you travel

Questions the source does — and does not — answer.

What are the dates?

No calendar dates are currently published. The program runs Friday through Monday.

What is the room arrangement?

Private/shared occupancy, bed assignment, and bathroom arrangements are not stated.

Are airport transfers included?

The source includes ground transportation “during the retreat.” It does not establish airport pickup or arrival/departure transfers.

What is the cancellation policy?

ITA currently publishes conflicting generic cancellation schedules. The retreat-specific governing policy requires written confirmation. Review the current ITA terms ↗ and confirm the applicable version directly with ITA at travel@itaglobal.com or 213-293-6422 before committing travel.

Is this medical care?

No. The retreat is not presented as individualized medical treatment.
