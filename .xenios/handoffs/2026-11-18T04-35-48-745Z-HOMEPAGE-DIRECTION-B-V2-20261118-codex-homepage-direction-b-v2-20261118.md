# Direction B expanded parent and anchored health experience

The parent keeps the maker line, approved mark and local Inter Tight/JetBrains Mono/Instrument Serif fonts. Light surfaces, ink and restrained purple retain the visual family. A shorter editorial hero exposes the current request action early. The complete health story uses #health, without a new /health route or shared Gateway edit. Infinity has a separate article.

Three original hero directions:

1. Selected parent: A clearer path to health. More possibility for your work. It preserves both offerings; the lead names categories and software development stage.
2. Health-led alternative: Clinical and wellness inquiries. Laboratory research, clearly explained. Better suited to a future standalone health route.
3. Process-led alternative: Know what you are exploring. Understand your next step. Useful support language, but less specific as the main offer.
